OUR COMMUNITY

Facilities & shared spaces.

Kaveri Unnatii Apartment is a residential community developed by Kaveri Builders, Davangere, located in Basaveshwara Layout, Davangere, Karnataka. The community consists of 35 residential homes and is a RERA-registered residential project.

More than just a place to live, Kaveri Unnatii has grown into a close-knit and friendly community where neighbours know one another, families celebrate together and residents share a strong sense of belonging.

Our Community

Our community reflects a warm and friendly cultural environment where residents come together to celebrate festivals, organize community activities and create lasting memories. From Ganesh Chaturthi, Sankranti and Holi celebrations to community gatherings and special occasions, our residents actively participate and contribute to making life at Kaveri Unnatii enjoyable and memorable.

We believe that an apartment becomes a true home not only because of its walls and facilities, but because of the people, relationships, values and memories created within it.

Our Community Values

  • Friendship & Togetherness — Building strong relationships among residents.
  • Family-Friendly Environment — A welcoming community for families and children.
  • Cultural Celebrations — Preserving traditions and celebrating festivals together.
  • Responsible Living — Keeping our surroundings clean, safe and well maintained.
  • Community Spirit — Supporting one another and working together for the betterment of our apartment.
  • Safety & Security — Maintaining a secure and comfortable environment for all residents.

35 Homes • One Community

Every family contributes something unique to Kaveri Unnatii, making it more than a residential project — it is a place we proudly call home.

Our Vision

To maintain Kaveri Unnatii as a safe, clean, friendly and culturally vibrant community where every resident feels respected, connected and proud to call it home.
