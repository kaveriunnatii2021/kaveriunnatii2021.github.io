ABOUT KAVERI UNNATII

A community built on trust, responsibility & togetherness.

Kaveri Unnatii Apartment is a residential community where families come together to create a safe, peaceful, welcoming and well-managed place to call home.

🏡

Our Home

Kaveri Unnatii Apartment is a thoughtfully developed residential community by Kaveri Builders, under the leadership of Mr. Parameshwara Rao, located in Basaveshwara Layout, Kundavada Main Road, Davangere, Karnataka.

Established in 2021, Kaveri Unnatii brings together families in a welcoming residential environment built around comfort, safety, cleanliness, community and responsible living.

Our Purpose

The Kaveri Unnatii Apartment Association works collectively to support the effective management of our shared facilities and the overall well-being of residents.

  • Maintain common areas and essential facilities
  • Promote a safe and secure residential environment
  • Support transparent and responsible association management
  • Maintain cleanliness and responsible waste management
🛡️

Our Commitment

We strive to maintain high standards of transparency, accountability, safety, cleanliness and community responsibility.

  • Strengthen security and visitor management
  • Address residents' concerns responsibly and promptly
  • Organise community events and celebrations
  • Encourage cooperation, respect and harmony
OUR COMMUNITY

More than an apartment.

Kaveri Unnatii is more than just a residential building—it is a community of families and individuals working together. Through active participation, responsible communication and mutual cooperation, residents contribute to creating a welcoming, respectful and comfortable place to live.

We believe that a well-managed residential community is built through the collective efforts of its residents, association members, staff and service partners.

OUR FACILITIES

Facilities designed for comfortable living.

Well-maintained facilities and shared spaces that support the comfort, safety and convenience of everyone at Kaveri Unnatii.

🚗

Dedicated Car Parking

A separate dedicated car parking space is provided for each flat, offering residents convenient and organised parking within the apartment premises.

🛗

Passenger Lifts

The apartment is equipped with two passenger lifts, providing convenient access to the residential floors for residents and visitors.

💧

Water Facilities

The apartment has water storage and community water-management facilities to support a reliable water supply for residents.

📹

Security & CCTV

Security measures and CCTV infrastructure help monitor common areas and support a safe and secure residential environment.

🌿

Common Areas

Shared spaces and common areas are maintained to provide a clean, comfortable and welcoming environment for residents.

🏢

Association Management

The Apartment Association works collectively to manage common facilities, maintenance, security, community activities and residents' concerns.

📦

Courier & Deliveries

Delivery and courier services are managed with appropriate entry and access procedures to help maintain convenience and security.

🐦

Pigeon Safety Netting

Bird-safety netting has been installed in designated areas to help protect residents' spaces while maintaining a clean and comfortable environment.
